# Fibre World

Website for **Fibre World** — a panel beating, spray painting, and fibreglass
fabrication business in Malangalanga, Lilongwe, Malawi.

> Quality service with reasonable price. We make your vehicle and boat look
> better than new.

## About the business

Fibre World serves the panel beating, spray painting, and fibreglass &
plastic repair industries. Beyond repairs, they design and fabricate
fibreglass process equipment and carports in-house.

**Products manufactured:**
- Steel-frame and cantilever carports, any colour and size
- Fibreglass process tanks, ducting, downcomers, and clarifiers
- Translucent fibreglass roof sheets
- Fibreglass jacuzzis, seats, and fittings

**Services offered:**
Panel beating · spray painting (including full respray) · fibreglass and
plastic repairs · dent and bumper repairs · gas and arc welding · plastic
welding · scratch repairs and buffing · windscreen fitments · mechanical
repairs · boat and vehicle upholstery · colour changes

**Location & hours:** Malangalanga, Lilongwe — weekdays, 8:00 AM–4:30 PM

## What this site does

A one-page marketing site: hero, mission, product and service showcase, a
photo gallery of finished work, and a quote-request form that submits to a
small backend and is saved for the business to follow up on.

## Stack

- **Frontend:** React + Vite
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express (handles the contact/quote form)

```
fibreworld/
```
